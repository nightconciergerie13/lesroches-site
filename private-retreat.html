What exclusive use means here

Les Roches is not a hotel with a function room. It is six independent houses set on the same hillside, each with its own kitchen, two bedrooms, two bathrooms and an infinity pool facing the ocean. Taking the place means taking all six at once.

There is no communal building, and we would rather say so now than let you find out on arrival. So we build one. Your group picks the house that becomes the common house: where you cook together, lay the long table, and gather once the sun has gone. It keeps its bedrooms if there are twenty-four of you. If you would rather have a real living space, we leave it free and you sleep twenty.

The rest follows: one person to talk to, one invoice, and the whole place yours for the length of the stay.

Four ways to live Les Roches

Yoga or wellness retreat

Six terraces facing the Pacific, sunrise over the jungle, and no other guests on the property. The common house takes the meals, the terraces take the practice. We arrange the chef, the groceries and the transfers so the only thing left to you is the programme.

There is no enclosed room and no purpose-built yoga deck: practice happens on the terraces, in the morning shade.

Family and friends

Twenty-four people, six houses, everyone with their own space and one table in the evening. It is what the place is most naturally made for: grandparents in the upper villas, reached without a single step, children on the garden level, and nobody in anybody's way.

The pools are infinity-edge and have no fence: with young children, supervision stays yours at every moment.

Intimate wedding

A ceremony facing the ocean, a seated dinner on the common terrace, and your people staying on site rather than scattered across the bay. This is a wedding for thirty, not a hundred, and that is precisely where its value lies.

Thirty people in total, residents included, and music until 8 pm: the neighbours are protected by quiet hours we keep.

Company offsite

Ten to twenty people, a week away from the office, and the kind of setting that changes what gets said in a meeting. High-speed fibre in every villa, and the common house turned into a workspace by day.

No equipped meeting room and no projector, and the line can drop: plan a phone hotspot for the video calls that cannot fail.

What to know before you ask

We would rather you knew everything now. A group that arrives informed has a better stay than a group that was spared the details.

  • Thirty people maximum on site: twenty-four in residence, plus up to six visitors passing through.
  • Music from 9 am to 8 pm, quiet from 8 pm to 8 am. The hillside has neighbours, and we care about them.
  • A 4x4 is needed for the climb, especially in the rainy season. Half the road is unpaved.
  • Better to have a vehicle: the beach is 6 to 7 minutes down by car, or a 20 to 30 minute walk with a hard climb back up.
  • The pools are unheated and unfenced. They are serviced three times a week.
  • Kitchens have no oven and no barbecue: hobs only, and a private chef if you would rather not think about meals.
  • End-of-stay cleaning included, with visits during the stay depending on its length. Surface cleaning, dishes not included.
  • Tap water is not drinkable: every villa has its own drinking water dispenser.

What we arrange for you

A group of twenty-four is not improvised on arrival. Everything below is settled before you land, with one person to talk to.

  • Private chef: we agree the number of meals, the style and your preferences together.
  • Pre-stocked fridges waiting on arrival, to be requested at least two days ahead.
  • Airport transfers from San José or Tambor, for the whole group.
  • 4x4 and quad rentals, arranged in advance rather than scrambled for on the day.
  • Massages, surf lessons, excursions: the Night concierge team builds the programme if you want one.
