Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy is also available in French and Spanish.

1. Who we are

The website lesrochesretreat.com is published and operated by NIGHT CONCIERGERIE SRL, a limited liability company under Costa Rican law, cédula jurídica 3-102-870510, with its registered office in Rohrmoser, Pavas, San José, Costa Rica. It manages six private villas in Santa Teresa under the Les Roches Retreat name. References to "we", "us" or "our" in this policy refer to NIGHT CONCIERGERIE SRL, the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR.

Contact: hello@lesrochesretreat.com, Calle Vista De Olas, Santa Teresa, Puntarenas, Costa Rica.

2. What data we collect

We collect personal data only when you voluntarily provide it. This includes:

  • Booking requests: your name, email address, phone number, desired dates and number of guests.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: your email address.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Visitor analytics: page visited, device type and referral source, counted by our own system. If you accept measurement, we also load Google Analytics 4, which is a third-party service and sets its own cookies, and we call ipapi.co once to establish the approximate country of the visit. See section 7.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (health information, financial data, identity documents) through this website.

3. How we use your data

  • Booking requests: to respond to your enquiry, confirm availability and finalize your reservation via WhatsApp and email.
  • Newsletter: to send occasional updates on availability, special offers and news about Les Roches. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Analytics: to understand how visitors use our website and improve our content.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): processing necessary to handle your booking request.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): anonymous analytics to improve our service.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): newsletter subscriptions. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase (infrastructure hosted in the EU). Booking and contact messages are transmitted by EmailJS, which sends them by email and does not keep them. Availability and reservations for the villas are handled by Beds24. Card payments are processed by ONVO through a payment endpoint we operate ourselves: your card number is never seen, transmitted or stored by us.

This website is published as static files on GitHub Pages.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

6. Data retention

  • Booking enquiries: retained for 3 years after your last interaction with us.
  • Newsletter emails: retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Analytics data: retained for 13 months on a rolling basis.

7. Cookies and measurement

On your first visit a consent banner appears. Until you answer it, nothing but strictly necessary storage is set.

If you accept measurement, we load Google Analytics 4 (property G-WFWBDDTMF0). It is a third-party service and it sets its own cookies. We use Google Consent Mode: before your acceptance, advertising and analytics storage are denied by default, and they are enabled only if you accept.

If you accept, we also make one call to ipapi.co to establish the approximate country of your visit. That call transmits your IP address to that service. Without your acceptance, the visit is counted without the country.

We run no advertising pixel and no remarketing. You may change your choice at any time from the banner.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability, receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@lesrochesretreat.com. We answer within the time limits set out in section 9 bis.

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (e.g. CNIL in France, AEPD in Spain, ICO in the UK).

8 bis. Costa Rican law

NIGHT CONCIERGERIE SRL is established in Costa Rica. The processing of your personal data is therefore also governed by Ley N.° 8968, on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of their personal data, and by its implementing regulation.

Under that law you may ask us to access, rectify or delete the data we hold about you, and you may withdraw a consent you have given. The supervisory authority is the Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes (PRODHAB), to which you may also address a complaint.

Where Costa Rican law and the GDPR differ, we apply whichever gives you the stronger protection.

9. Third-party services

  • Supabase, data storage (infrastructure hosted in the EU)
  • EmailJS, transmission of booking and contact messages
  • Beds24, availability and reservations
  • ONVO, card payments
  • Google Analytics 4, measurement, loaded only after your consent (Google LLC, USA)
  • Google Fonts, font delivery (Google LLC, USA)
  • ipapi.co, approximate country of the visit, called only after your consent
  • Behold, display of our Instagram feed
  • GitHub Pages, hosting of this website
  • Flatpickr, date picker (loaded locally, no external request)

9 bis. International transfers, deadlines and children

Transfers outside your country. Several of the services listed above are established outside Costa Rica and outside the European Union, in particular in the United States: Google (Analytics and Fonts), EmailJS, ipapi.co, Behold and GitHub. Using this website therefore involves a transfer of data to those countries. For transfers from the European Union we rely on the standard contractual clauses published by the European Commission, which those providers incorporate in their terms. Under Costa Rican law, your acceptance of this policy constitutes your consent to that transfer.

How quickly we answer. A request made under the GDPR is answered within one month. A request made under Ley 8968 is answered within five working days, as that law provides. If a request is complex we will tell you, and say when you will have our answer.

Children. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. A booking is made by an adult, who is responsible for the guests in their party.

No automated decisions. We take no decision about you by automated means alone, and we carry out no profiling.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
