Getting to Santa Teresa from San José

Santa Teresa is 150 km from San José airport, but there is a ferry in the middle. Here are the four ways to arrive, what they really cost, and which one to pick depending on when you land.

The plane, the fastest

A domestic flight of about 30 minutes to Cóbano, the airstrip closest to Santa Teresa, then a 30-minute drive. Two carriers fly the route: Fly Sansa and Costa Rica Green Airways.

What to know: the aircraft seats about a dozen people and luggage is limited to roughly 15 kg. The last flight leaves around 5pm, the first of the next day around 6:40am. We arrange the Cóbano to villa transfer for $50.

The private transfer, the simplest

A driver picks you up at the airport and drops you at the villa, ferry included: he boards with you and drives the last stretch on the other side. Around $350 for one to six people, air conditioning, water on board, child seat on request.

A two-leg version costs around $250: a first driver to Puntarenas port, you cross the ferry on foot, a second taxi waits on the other side.

The shared shuttle, the cheapest

Daily departures, 5 to 6 hours with several drop-offs along the way, $55 to $75 per person. Two reliable operators: Easy Ride and Tropical Tours Shuttles.

The car, the freest

About 5 hours via the Paquera ferry, including an hour of crossing. Departures are roughly two hours apart: check the timetable on the day, it is what makes the total time vary.

Reliable rental companies: Adobe, Vamos, Budget. $80 to $150 a day. Book early in high season, December to the end of January: fleets sell out and prices climb.

Do you need a 4×4?

Yes, from May to November. Santa Teresa's roads are dirt, including the main strip, and the side roads flood briefly during green season. In dry season, December to April, a two-wheel-drive SUV is enough.

If you land after 4pm

The last flight to Cóbano has gone, and the road at night is not advisable. The wisest move is to sleep near the airport and take the morning flight. Airport hotels run $100 to $170 a night, shuttle included.

And from Liberia (LIR)?

Simpler on paper: 4 to 5 hours of road, no ferry, via highway 1 then 18. Private transfer $320-380, shared shuttle $70-90 per person but departures are rare, self-drive 4h30. There is no convenient air route from Liberia.

Once you are here

There is no Uber in Santa Teresa, but reliable local drivers whose numbers we pass to our guests. The ATV is the local habit, delivered to the villa with helmets, $85 a day. It is not the vehicle for coming from the airport, nor for heavy rain: the right combination is a car to arrive and an ATV to get around.
