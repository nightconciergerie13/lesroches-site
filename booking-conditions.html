Cancellation & conditions

Our booking policy

💳
Paid in full when you book
The full amount of your stay, taxes included, is charged by card at the moment of booking. There is no deposit and no balance to settle on arrival.
✦
Cancel until 60 days before arrival — 96% back
Cancel at any time up to 60 days before your arrival date and 96% of your stay is refunded to the card you paid with, no explanation needed. The remaining 4% is the card processing fee: our payment provider keeps it whatever happens, so we cannot return it. Booking direct still saves you 5% — more than a cancellation would ever cost you.
🕗
Cancelling less than 60 days before arrival
Between 60 and 30 days before arrival, half of the amount paid is refunded and we keep the other half. Less than 30 days before arrival, no refund is made.
💬
How to cancel
Write to us on WhatsApp or by email with your booking reference. We confirm the cancellation and start the refund the same day. Refunds are issued to the original card and usually appear within a few working days.
Terms

Booking terms

1. Who you are booking with

NIGHT CONCIERGERIE SRL, a limited liability company under Costa Rican law, cédula jurídica 3-102-870510, registered office in Rohrmoser, Pavas, San José. It operates the six villas known as Les Roches Retreat, Calle Vista De Olas, 60111, province of Puntarenas. Contact: hello@lesrochesretreat.com.

2. What you are booking

An entire villa, not a room. Each villa has two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an open kitchen and its own pool, and sleeps four people at most. The six villas together sleep twenty-four.

3. Price and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars, per night, taxes included. There is no cleaning fee and no service fee. The full amount is charged to your card at the moment of booking, through our payment provider ONVO. Your card number is never seen, transmitted or stored by us.

4. Minimum stay

It depends on the dates: most often two nights, sometimes three in busy periods, occasionally one. The calendar on each villa page applies the exact minimum for the dates you choose and tells you what it is.

5. Cancellation

Up to 60 days before arrival, 96 % of the amount paid is refunded to the card used; the remaining 4 % is the card processing fee, which our payment provider keeps in all cases. Between 60 and 30 days before arrival, half the amount paid is refunded. Less than 30 days before arrival, no refund is made. To cancel, write to us by email or WhatsApp with your booking reference.

6. Arrival and departure

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. We welcome you in person and adjust these times where we can.

7. Occupancy and use

The villa is for the guests declared at booking, four at most. Parties and events are not held in the villas without our prior written agreement; the whole property can be privatised for that purpose. We ask you to treat your neighbours' quiet as you would want yours treated.

8. Dogs

One dog per villa, declared when you book. No size limit, no weight limit, no supplement. Your dog is welcome on the terrace and around the pool. The land is not fenced.

9. The pools

Each villa has its own pool. The pools are not fenced, they are not supervised, and they hold a single depth throughout. Children must be within reach of an adult at all times. You swim at your own risk.

10. Access and the road

The property sits on a hillside. The access road is unpaved and unlit, and a vehicle is strongly recommended. In the green season, from May to November, a car with good ground clearance is advisable.

11. Damage

You are responsible for damage caused during your stay by you or by anyone you bring. We will tell you about it and document it before asking anything of you.

12. Our liability

We are liable for what we owe you under this agreement. We are not liable for events outside our control: weather, power or water interruptions, road conditions, or the acts of third parties.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our fault, for fraud, or for any liability that Costa Rican consumer law (Ley 7472) does not permit to be excluded or limited.

13. Force majeure

If an event beyond the control of either party prevents the stay, we will agree together on a postponement or on a refund of the amounts paid for the nights not taken.

14. Your personal data

Processed as described in our Privacy Policy.

15. Complaints

Write to hello@lesrochesretreat.com and we will answer. In Costa Rica, a consumer may also bring a matter before the Comisión Nacional del Consumidor of the Ministry of Economy, Industry and Commerce (MEIC).

16. Applicable law

This agreement is governed by Costa Rican law, and the competent Costa Rican courts have jurisdiction. If you are a consumer resident in the European Union, this choice does not deprive you of the protection afforded by the mandatory rules of your country of residence, and you keep the right to bring proceedings before the courts of that country where its law so provides.

17. Who may book

The person making the booking must be of legal age and books on behalf of every guest in the party, whose compliance with these terms they guarantee.

18. If you do not arrive, or leave early

If you do not arrive and have not cancelled, the cancellation terms above apply as at the date of arrival. Nights not taken after an early departure are not refunded.

19. If we have to cancel

If we cannot make the villa available, we offer you another villa of the same standard on the property or, failing that, we refund you in full within fourteen days. Where the cancellation is our doing, this does not prevent you from claiming any further loss the law allows you to claim.

20. Change of villa

If a villa becomes unusable, we may house you in another villa of the same standard on the property. If you prefer not to accept it, the nights concerned are refunded.

21. Smoking

Smoking is not permitted inside the villas. It is tolerated on the terraces, with care: the vegetation is dry from January to April.

22. Lost property

We keep items found after your departure for thirty days, and we return them at your expense.

23. Which version applies

The terms in force at the moment you book govern that booking. Later changes apply only to later bookings.

24. What the agreement consists of

These terms and your booking confirmation form the whole of the agreement between us. Where the two differ on the dates, the villa or the price, the confirmation prevails.

25. Language

These terms exist in English, French and Spanish. Where the versions diverge, the one in the language in which you made your booking prevails.

26. If a clause fails

If any clause is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining clauses continue to apply.
