Six pools, six villas

A private pool, not the hotel's

In Santa Teresa, most places with an ocean view share one pool between every guest. At Les Roches there is no shared pool at all: each of the six villas has its own, infinity edge, facing the Pacific.

You never book a slot

You do not reserve a time, you do not walk past anyone, you do not lower your voice. The pool belongs to the house, the same way the kitchen does. It is the one thing we deliberately did not share.

The six are identical

Same basin for every villa: about 15 m², infinity edge, the lip giving straight onto the ocean. There is no difference on this point between the upper level and the garden level.

They are not heated, and they do not need to be: the water stays at a good temperature all year. The sun reaches the pool around 10am and stays on it through the afternoon. They are lit at night.

What you should know

The depth is the same from end to end and is meant for adults. There is no fence around the water: with young children, supervision remains yours at every moment. We would rather write it here than let you discover it on arrival.

Pool towels are provided, and the pools are serviced three times a week.

What comes with the villa

Two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an open kitchen, air conditioning, high-speed WiFi, daily housekeeping except Sunday, private parking. Four guests per villa, twenty-four if you take all six.
